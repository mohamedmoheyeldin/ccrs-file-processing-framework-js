**Release Update — CCRS File Processing Framework v1.5.3**

- Correct dropdown values: Stations, Networks, and SEOC values follow your selections. Payment Authority Codes use the team’s 43 unique codes and no longer increment.
- Simpler choices: Referral Number offers VA or Custom; ICN offers Default, Custom, or Referral Number. Payment codes use a searchable, numbers-only dropdown.
- Flexible output: Keep individual JSON files, a combined master JSON, or just .gz. Matching filenames are overwritten after validation.
- Current dates: New referrals use current dates; expiration follows Referral Period. Custom dates remain available.
- Improved uploads: Servers are handled one at a time, duplicate destinations are avoided, and the queue continues if a server fails.
- Easier to use: Compact date controls, scrollable dialogs at higher zoom, and automatic output validation. Generate Manifest is optional after completion.
